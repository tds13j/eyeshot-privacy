EYESHOT – Child Safety Standards

App package: com.eyeshot.app

Response commitment: all reports are reviewed within 24 hours

Who this app is for

EYESHOT is for adults, 18 and over. It exists to help people who are already in the same place decide whether to speak to each other. It is not intended for, marketed to, or usable by minors.

Keeping minors out

  • Store age signals. We use the age information supplied by the app stores. If the store reports a minor, the account cannot be created.
  • Restrict Declared Minors. Enabled in Google Play Console, so users whose declared age indicates under 18 cannot access the app.
  • Declared date of birth. Required at sign-up; under-18 is refused.
  • Reporting. Every profile carries a report control with "Appears to be under 18" as its own category, reviewed ahead of all other reports.

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material

We prohibit any content that sexualises minors. Accounts found to be involved are removed permanently and without warning, and reported to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the relevant authorities in Greece and the EU. One report is enough; we do not require a pattern or a threshold.

Reporting to us

  • In the app: profile card or chat menu → Report
  • Email: t_d_s1@hotmail.com

Reports about suspected minors, sexual content involving minors, or grooming are treated as urgent and looked at ahead of everything else.

What happens to a report

  1. Received and timestamped
  2. The reported account is hidden from the reporter immediately, before review
  3. Reviewed within 24 hours
  4. Confirmed violations: permanent removal, and referral to authorities where the law requires it
  5. The reporter is told the outcome

Design decisions that reduce risk

  • Proximity only. Nobody can browse a city, a school, or any area they are not physically in.
  • Mutual consent to contact. No message is possible unless both people independently chose each other.
  • Silent rejection. Interest that is not returned is never revealed, so there is no channel for pressure or repeated approaches.
  • Short-lived data. Presence expires, and no location history is kept.
